# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or affiliation.

Expected behavior includes:

- Using respectful and inclusive language
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback
- Focusing on the technical issue rather than the person
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and repairing harm

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized language or attention, publishing private information, and any conduct that would make a reasonable person feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting

Report harassment or other unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository's GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive personal information or proprietary source code in a report.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.
